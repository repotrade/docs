## Repository Tokenization Process

Our community members are actively involved in all stages of the Repo.trade process:

1. **Pick a Repo** - Browse open source projects you want to support or trade
2. **Send USDC** - Join the 2-week auction where 500M tokens (50% of total supply) are distributed based on your contributions
3. **Claim Tokens** - After the auction, claim your share based on how much USDC you sent each day
4. **Trade & Earn** - Repository owners receive 40% of tokens plus collected USDC as initial liquidity, and 50% of all trading fees
